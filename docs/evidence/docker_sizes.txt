3. GIAI TRINH NGUYEN NHAN CHENH LECH (TECHNICAL EXPLANATION)
=====================================================================================
1. OpenJDK JDK vs JRE Headless (Lop OS Package):
   - Baseline cai dat openjdk-11-jdk (511 MB) bao gom ca compiler (javac), debug tools va GUI/X11.
   - Single-stage va Multistage chi cai openjdk-11-jre-headless (211 MB) kem apt purge va rm apt/lists/*.
   - Muc tiet kiem: ~300 MB chi o tang he dieu hanh.

2. Pip Cache vs --no-cache-dir (Lop Python Packages):
   - Baseline cai dat bang pip khong co --no-cache-dir (1.47 GB), luu toan bo file .whl va .tar.gz
     tai /home/airflow/.cache/pip.
   - Single-stage su dung --no-cache-dir (720 MB), giam 750 MB nho loai bo cache trung gian.

3. So sanh Single-Stage Clean vs Multistage Build:
   - Ca 2 bien the deu co dung luong them vao tuong duong (~931 MB) va tong dung luong CLI 3.52 GB.
   - Ly do: Builder stage su dung cung base image apache/airflow:2.7.3-python3.10 va khong co
     trinh bien dich C (gcc/make) can duoc loai bo khoi runtime. Thu muc /home/airflow/.local
     do builder sinh ra co kich thuoc giong het single-stage pip install.
   - Ket luan: Multistage dong vai tro tach biet kien truc (Builder Pattern) giup tach quy trinh
     build va runtime, nhung khong giam them dung luong so voi single-stage duoc don dep dung cach.
